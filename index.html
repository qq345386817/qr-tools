QR code questions

Are these QR codes free and static?

The website generates free static QR codes without an account. The content is stored in the QR image itself, not behind a QR Tools redirect. Changing the content requires generating and sharing a new image.

Does QR Tools upload or save my data?

QR content, Wi-Fi passwords and selected images are processed in your browser. The website does not upload them or save a history. Downloaded or shared QR images contain the information you entered.

Should I download a PNG or SVG QR code?

PNG is useful for image sharing and documents. SVG stays sharp when resized for printing. Both encode the same content; keep the light margin around the code and use a dark code on a light background.

Can the website capture another browser tab?

Paste the page URL into the website to create its QR code. The Safari and Chrome extensions can obtain the current tab URL directly; the website cannot read your other tabs.
