    QR code questions

    What is included in a contact QR code?

    A contact QR code encodes a vCard 3.0 record with the name and optional phone number, email, organization and website you enter. A compatible scanner can offer to add those details to contacts.

    Can I update a contact card after distributing it?

    This is a static vCard QR code, not a hosted digital profile. To change a phone number or other details, generate a new image and replace the previously distributed code.

    Which contact details should I share?

    Include only the information you intend recipients to receive. QR images can be copied and decoded by anyone who has them. No account is required, and the website does not save your contact card.
