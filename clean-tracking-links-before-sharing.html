Cleaner QR sharing

Clean tracking links before sharing as a QR code

Long URLs often include tracking parameters. QR Tools can remove common tracking parameters before creating a QR code, making the shared link easier to read and scan.

What Clean URL does

When enabled, QR Tools removes common campaign and click-tracking parameters such as UTM-style fields before generating the QR code. You can review and edit the QR content before copying or saving the image.

Why cleaner links matter

Shorter QR content

Cleaner URLs often generate simpler QR codes that are easier to scan from screens and slides.

Better meeting handoff

Share the useful page URL without unnecessary campaign metadata attached to the QR code.

Local generation

QR Tools generates QR images on your device. The content you paste or capture from the current browser tab is not uploaded to a server.

Try QR Tools

Use Clean URL from the Safari or Chrome extension popup before copying or saving a QR image.
