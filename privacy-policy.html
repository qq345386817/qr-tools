Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2024

Overview

QR Tools creates QR codes from content you provide or from the active browser tab. QR generation is local and does not require a user account.

Personal information

QR Tools does not require your name, email address, or account information to generate QR codes. The app and extension do not collect keystrokes or unrelated device activity.

Local data

The extension may use local browser storage for settings. The native app may store recent QR content locally so you can reuse it.

Analytics

QR Tools does not use Google Analytics or similar analytics services.
