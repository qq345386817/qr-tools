Cross-device browsing

Send a desktop link to iPhone with a QR code

QR Tools helps you move the page you are viewing from Safari or Chrome to iPhone without AirDrop, chat messages, email drafts, or browser sync.

The fast handoff flow

  1. Open the page on your Mac or iPad.
  2. Click QR Tools in Safari or Chrome.
  3. Scan the generated QR code with your iPhone camera.
  4. Open the page immediately on your phone.

When it helps

Mobile testing

Move a staging page or responsive layout to iPhone quickly during development or QA.

Personal browsing

Continue reading, shopping, researching, or navigating on your phone without sending the URL to yourself.

Private by default

The QR code is generated locally. QR Tools does not require an account, and your QR content is not uploaded to a server.

Install QR Tools

Use it as a Safari extension on Apple devices, or as a Chrome extension when Chrome is your desktop browser.
