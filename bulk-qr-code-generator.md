# Bulk QR Code Generator from CSV & Printable Labels

Bulk QR code generator from CSV: preview up to 100 labels, download PNG/SVG ZIP and print on A4 or Letter. Local processing without an account.

[Bulk QR Code Generator from CSV & Printable Labels](https://qrtools.luopeike.com/bulk-qr-code-generator)

## What does this tool create?

Bulk QR code generator from CSV: preview up to 100 labels, download PNG/SVG ZIP and print on A4 or Letter. Local processing without an account.

## Is my input uploaded or saved?

No. Input and output are processed in this browser, without an account or persistent history. Downloaded files contain the data you entered.

## What are the limitations?

The batch is limited to 100 codes and a 1 MB CSV. Printing uses eight labels per sheet. Test a printed label before distributing the batch.
