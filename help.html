Help

Enable the Safari extension

Open QR Tools, choose Safari Settings, then enable QR Tools in Safari Extensions.

Create a QR code from the current page

Open Safari or Chrome, visit the page you want to share, then open QR Tools from the browser toolbar. The active page URL appears automatically.

Edit, clean, copy, or save

Edit the content before generating the QR code. Use Clean URL to remove common tracking parameters, then copy the QR image or save it as a PNG.

Use native templates

The app can generate Link, Wi-Fi, Email, SMS, and Contact QR content. Recent QR content is stored locally for quick reuse.
